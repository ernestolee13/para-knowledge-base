Local entrypoint for a fictional active project.

## Purpose

## Key Notes

- [[Customer Interview Synthesis]] - Recurring objections, feature requests, and adoption signals.

## Query Hints

- For risk questions, read `Risk Register` and only then inspect recent log entries.
